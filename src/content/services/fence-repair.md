## We check the posts first

A fence is only as good as what holds it up. Boards and panels are the part you see, but the post is the part that decides whether this is a repair or a replacement. So that is where we start, before anyone quotes you a whole new fence line.

- **Posts solid, boards bad.** That is a repair, and a fast one.
- **One or two posts gone, rest of the line fine.** Reset or replace those posts and rehang the sections.
- **Posts failing down the whole run.** We will tell you. Bolting new panels to rotten posts is money you spend twice.

Leaning posts after a wet spring. Gates that stopped latching once the ground moved. Pickets that went soft at the bottom where the mulch sits against them. Sections that came down in a thunderstorm and are sitting in the yard waiting on somebody.
