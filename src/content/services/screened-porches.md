## Deck first, or porch first

If you already have a deck that is structurally sound, converting it is usually the best money you can spend outside. A porch roof loads the structure very differently than people and furniture do, so the first thing we check is whether the footings, posts, and joists were built for it. Many were not, and we would rather say that at the estimate than after the roof is up.

If you are starting from nothing, we build it as a porch from the ground up. That is simpler and often not much more expensive than converting a deck that needs reframing anyway.

## The roof is the whole job

Where the porch roof meets the house is the detail that decides whether you love this porch or fight it. Pitch, flashing, and the tie-in have to send water away from the wall, not into it. A porch that leaks at the house is not a porch problem, it is a roofline problem, and it is why this part gets the attention.

## What it looks like finished

A finished ceiling instead of exposed framing. A screen door that closes on its own. Composite or wood flooring you can hose off. A fan box already framed in, so the fan goes up on day one instead of becoming a project.
