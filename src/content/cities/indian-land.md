## Handyman and Remodeling in Indian Land, SC

Indian Land is the panhandle of Lancaster County, running along Highway 521 between Ballantyne and Lancaster. It is one of the fastest growing parts of the Charlotte metro, which means a lot of homes built in the same decade and now needing the same repairs at the same time. From **Sun City Carolina Lakes** to **BridgeMill** and **Walnut Creek**, iFIXX is a short drive from our South Charlotte territory.
